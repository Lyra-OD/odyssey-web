## 1. PURPOSE

The Parties wish to exchange certain information in connection with discussions regarding a potential commercial, technological or strategic partnership concerning Odyssey, its products, its services and its technology platform (the “**Purpose**”).

In the course of those discussions, the Disclosing Party may disclose to the Receiving Party certain confidential, commercial, technological and strategic information.

This Agreement is intended to protect that information and to strictly govern its use.

---

## 2. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

For the purposes of this Agreement, “**Confidential Information**” includes, without limitation:

- Odyssey’s source code, software code, scripts and technical elements;
- technical and software architecture, infrastructure and operating methods;
- mockups, designs, UI/UX interfaces, user journeys and digital experiences;
- Odyssey’s current or future features;
- proprietary concepts, methods, processes and mechanisms developed by Odyssey, including the “Coffre”, the “Sanctuaire” and the “Film de sa vie”;
- any Odyssey trademark or trade name;
- the B2B2C business model, including specifically the “Freemium” acquisition strategy (base plan offered free of charge to partners);
- the family pricing incentive structure (including the conversion funnel toward paid plans such as “Héritage” or “Éternité”);
- financial mechanisms and revenue-share percentages (“RevShare”) contemplated for B2B partners;
- commercial, marketing, distribution and development strategies;
- information relating to customers, partners, suppliers or prospects;
- documents and other materials provided by the Disclosing Party;
- any information that, given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure, should reasonably be considered confidential;
- and any analysis, adaptation, synthesis or derivation made from such information.

Demonstrations, presentations, temporary platform access, demo accounts, prototypes and features presented to the Receiving Party are deemed Confidential Information, whether or not accompanied by a written document.

Confidential Information may be communicated verbally, visually, electronically, in writing or by any other means. For rigorous handling, documents sent in writing or by email should ideally bear the legend “**CONFIDENTIAL**”, provided that the absence of that legend does not mean information ceases to be Confidential Information if it meets this definition.

---

## 3. EXCLUSIONS

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

a) was publicly available at the time of disclosure;

b) became public without breach of this Agreement;

c) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;

d) was lawfully obtained from a third party not bound by a confidentiality obligation; or

e) must be disclosed pursuant to a legal obligation or an order of a competent authority, in which case the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted, give prior notice to the Disclosing Party.

For greater certainty, the fact that a concept, product category or commercial practice is generally known in an industry does not exclude from confidentiality Odyssey’s particular combination, assembly, execution or commercialization (including Coffre, Sanctuaire, Film de sa vie, Freemium, RevShare and conversion journey) when communicated in connection with the Purpose.

---

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

The Receiving Party agrees to:

a) protect Confidential Information with a reasonable degree of care at least equivalent to that which it applies to its own confidential information of a similar nature;

b) not disclose Confidential Information to any unauthorized person or entity;

c) limit access to Confidential Information to its employees, officers, professional advisors, consultants or subcontractors who have a genuine need to know in connection with the Purpose;

d) ensure that such persons are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations;

e) remain responsible for any unauthorized use or disclosure by persons to whom it has given access to Confidential Information.

The Receiving Party may not rely on the fact that Confidential Information was memorized, rather than retained in written or electronic form, to circumvent the confidentiality and non-use obligations hereunder.

---

## 5. NON-USE

The Receiving Party agrees to use Confidential Information solely to evaluate, negotiate or, if applicable, implement the contemplated partnership in connection with the Purpose.

The Receiving Party shall not, directly or indirectly:

- reproduce Confidential Information for purposes unrelated to the Purpose;
- use Confidential Information to develop, have developed, finance, market or operate a product, software, platform or service competing with Odyssey;
- transmit Confidential Information to a developer, agency, consultant, supplier or any other third party for the purpose of reproducing or developing a solution based thereon;
- use proprietary concepts, features, methods, processes, architectures, designs or strategies communicated by the Disclosing Party to create a competing or substantially similar solution;
- reproduce, draw inspiration from or appropriate the financial models, pricing structures (Freemium) or revenue-share mechanisms (RevShare) disclosed by the Disclosing Party to structure a similar service offering for its own clients, network or with the assistance of a third-party developer;
- engage in any act of unfair competition, including by drawing the public’s attention to its own products, services or business in a manner likely to cause confusion with those of Odyssey.

This clause addresses use of Confidential Information disclosed in connection with the Purpose and is not a general prohibition on the Receiving Party carrying on its legitimate business activities.

---

## 6. NON-CIRCUMVENTION

For the duration of the non-circumvention obligations under this Agreement, the Receiving Party shall not use Confidential Information or information obtained in connection with the Purpose to circumvent Odyssey with respect to a business opportunity, customer, partner, supplier or business relationship that was specifically presented, introduced or identified to it by Odyssey in the course of the discussions, and of which it had no prior knowledge.

The Receiving Party also agrees not to use Confidential Information to establish directly with a third party a relationship or structure intended to reproduce or pursue, without the Disclosing Party’s participation, a business opportunity specifically presented by the Disclosing Party in connection with the Purpose, and of which the Receiving Party had no prior knowledge.

---

## 7. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

All Confidential Information remains the exclusive property of the Disclosing Party.

Nothing in this Agreement confers on the Receiving Party any right, licence or interest in Odyssey’s intellectual property rights, which remain the exclusive property of their respective owners.

This Agreement does not limit any rights or remedies Odyssey may have under applicable laws relating to intellectual property, trademarks, copyright, trade secrets or unfair competition.

Disclosure of Confidential Information does not constitute an assignment of intellectual property.

---

## 8. NO OBLIGATION TO ENTER INTO A PARTNERSHIP

This Agreement is not a promise, offer or obligation to enter into a partnership, transaction, licence, distribution arrangement or any other commercial relationship between the Parties.

Each Party remains free to end discussions at any time, subject to the obligations under this Agreement.

---

## 9. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF INFORMATION

At the Disclosing Party’s request, or at the end of discussions between the Parties, the Receiving Party shall, within a reasonable time:

a) return or destroy documents and materials containing Confidential Information;

b) delete electronic copies under its control, to the extent reasonably practicable;

c) cease all use of Confidential Information.

Upon request, the Receiving Party shall confirm in writing that it has complied with these obligations.

---

## 10. TERM

This Agreement takes effect on the date of signature.

Confidentiality and non-use obligations shall remain in force for a period of **three (3) years** following the last disclosure of Confidential Information in connection with the Purpose.

However, with respect to information constituting trade secrets or information that remains confidential under applicable law, confidentiality obligations shall remain in force for as long as such information retains its confidential character or constitutes a trade secret.

Non-circumvention obligations shall remain in force for a period of **three (3) years** following the last disclosure of Confidential Information in connection with the Purpose.

---

## 11. NO WARRANTY

Confidential Information is communicated in the course of discussions between the Parties.

Except as otherwise agreed in writing, the Disclosing Party gives no warranty as to the completeness or accuracy of Confidential Information communicated in exploratory discussions.

---

## 12. REMEDIES

The Parties acknowledge that unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information could cause significant harm to the Disclosing Party.

The Disclosing Party may exercise any remedy available under applicable law in respect of a breach of this Agreement, including, where the applicable conditions are met, seeking an injunction or any other measure to stop unauthorized use or disclosure.

---

## 13. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Québec and the laws of Canada applicable therein.

Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be submitted to the competent courts of Québec, subject to mandatory rules of jurisdiction.

---

## 14. ENTIRE AGREEMENT AND ELECTRONIC SIGNATURE

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the confidentiality of Confidential Information disclosed in connection with the Purpose and supersedes any prior discussion or agreement specifically on that subject.

Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by the Parties.

The Parties agree that this Agreement may be signed electronically (e.g. DocuSign, HelloSign) and that a secure electronic signature binds the Parties to the same extent as a traditional handwritten signature.

---
