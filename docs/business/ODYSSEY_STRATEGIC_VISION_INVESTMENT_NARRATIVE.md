**From analog services to the global infrastructure for human memory and end-of-life logistics.**

---

## 1. The Thesis: The Last Analog Market

The global death-care and memory market is a massive industry that remains fundamentally stuck in the analog era (USB drives, email chains, paper processes). As we enter the largest intergenerational wealth transfer in history, the decision-makers are digital natives. They manage their finances, health, and lives online, yet are forced into an outdated, high-friction logistical nightmare when dealing with the loss of a loved one. The disconnect is total. **Odyssey is building the definitive digital layer for that moment.**

---

## 2. Our Unfair Advantage: 5 Years in the Trenches

We are not outsiders guessing what this market needs. We have spent the last **5 years** operating inside the funeral industry—creating tribute videos and living the exact frictions families and funeral directors face daily. We know where operations break. We deploy product from **insider knowledge and existing B2B relationships**, not assumed personas.

---

## 3. The Playbook: A 3-Phase Ecosystem

To build a durable platform you need: **zero-friction acquisition**, a **scalable retention engine**, and a **global monetization layer**.

### Phase 1 — The Wedge (Odyssey Studio)

**Model (B2B2C):** Funeral homes offer Odyssey Studio to families as a **free** Souvenir experience—collect memories, collaborate, build the Memory Chest **at home** (not at the counter).

**Value**

| For the funeral home | For Odyssey |
|----------------------|-------------|
| Admin nightmare → streamlined gesture (~**30 seconds**) | Near-**$0 CAC** at peak need |
| New **profit center** (RevShare) | Captive, high-intent users via partner networks |

**Monetization (aligned with live commerce)**

```text
Souvenir $0 (gift)
  → Family builds Memory Chest at home
  → Soft Cap → paid packages (Heritage / Eternity) + Quiet Luxury add-ons
  → Platform fee 10% → Partner RevShare 30% of Net
  → Optional Memorial Fund / viral guest contributions (tenant flag)
```

*We do not sell aggressively at the funeral counter. Attachment happens later, at home.*

### Phase 2 — The Upside (Lyra + MRR)

Once Phase 1 captures the base, we deploy **Lyra**—AI orchestrator and data engine.

**Orchestrator:** Guided assistant for **directors and families**—post-death admin, checklists, cancellations (phone, internet, subscriptions…). **Humans seal final legal acts**; Lyra prepares and routes. Quiet Luxury, not autonomous “robot notary.”

**Retention & data:** Memory Chest → living generational asset · path to **Sanctuary MRR** · privacy-compliant **data graph** with **explicit, separate consent** (Law 25)—transactional upload ≠ marketing / graph opt-in.

**Margin path:** Higher-margin automation over time (media + ops). We do **not** claim agency-to-85%-SaaS overnight; cinema-grade production automation is a parallel roadmap, not the same as Lyra’s admin orchestrator.

### Phase 3 — The Endgame (The “Everything” Marketplace)

**Platform Play.** Amazon started with books; we start with the **tribute film and Memory Chest**. Phase 3 routes a captive, high-trust audience into a fragmented global supply chain—**take-rate / affiliation**, **no inventory**.

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Partner-first routing** | Leads for halls / cemetery plots **back to the acquiring funeral home** — ally, not competitor |
| **Global + local supply** | Manufacturers, artisans, curated catalog |
| **Long tail** | Ultra-niche innovations (illustrative; not the Phase 1 pitch) |

*We are **not** raising to build the marketplace first.*

## 4. Unit Economics (Phase 1 Baseline)

We do **not** need Phase 3 to be a viable business. The wedge is designed to generate cash early.

| Metric | Illustrative figure | Meaning |
|--------|---------------------|---------|
| **Partner commission / incoming family** | **~$22** Soft Cap only | Partner economics (not Odyssey top-line) |
| **Partner commission / incoming family** | **~$43** with viral / Fund | Near **2×** unit leverage |
| **Odyssey** | Residual after **10% fee**, **30% Net RevShare**, and **COGS** | Our share of the waterfall |

**Note:** Figures are **operational illustrations** (see Business Case V3 / partner script)—they prove the wedge before we scale Lyra or the Marketplace. Live package prices: FREEMIUM canon (Heritage **$179** · Eternity **$349**, etc.).

## 5. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **B2B activation** (directors resist change) | **30-second rule** · measured pilots · internal champions · no 15-minute “software training” |
| **Privacy / Law 25** (family data · Lyra graph) | Explicit **separate** consent gates · transactional ≠ data / marketing |
| **Export / ceremony-day trust** | Don’t over-promise cinema master until delivered · P0 gate first |
| **Lyra liability** | Human seals legal acts · QC-first scope · audit trail |
| **Marketplace dignity** | Curated · opt-in · partner-first · no day-of spam |
